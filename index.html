Colorado Thrives AI Fellows

Colorado Thrives pairs early-career technologists with Colorado employers, government agencies, and nonprofits for a 10-week AI fellowship. Each fellow spends a week of pre-work learning Claude, two days of live training in Denver scoping and prototyping with their host organization, then the rest of the summer building, testing, and handing off a working AI solution — with CodePath and Anthropic providing mentorship throughout.

This year's cohort of 14 fellows is closing out the program with a showcase on August 12, 2026 at McKinsey Denver. Below is what each fellow delivered for their host organization.
